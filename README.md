# About Me — Rengga Prakoso Nugroho

Rengga Prakoso Nugroho is a researcher in Educational Technology at  
[Teknologi Pendidikan ID](https://teknologipendidikan.or.id), working with the  
[Department of Educational Technology](https://tep-fip.um.ac.id/) with academic interests in learning strategies, instructional facilitation, and the design and implementation of learning management systems. His research focuses on technology-enhanced learning, evidence-based instructional design, and the relationship between learner behavior, digital platforms, and educational practice.
